Google Play listing copy (1.2.0)

App name (7/30)

SayCopy

Short description (78/80)

Speak or type, then transcribe or translate in 11 languages with your own key.

Full description

SayCopy turns your voice into clean text you can copy, share, and keep — or into a translation you can read aloud. Record up to three minutes and get a transcript back in seconds. Optional Light cleanup fixes punctuation, capitalization, and filler without changing what you meant or the language you spoke. On the Translate tab, type or speak in one language and get the text in another. Choose from English, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Hindi, or let Auto-detect figure out what you spoke. Listen to the result with your phone's own voices, and save the translations you want to keep. You bring your own OpenRouter API key and choose from the models available in SayCopy. The app is free, but selected models may charge usage to your OpenRouter account. Designed for a clear, focused workflow: • Record, stop, or cancel whenever you are ready • Transcribe speech with optional Light cleanup • Translate typed or spoken text between 11 languages • Listen to translations with on-device voices • Copy results or open the Android share sheet • Keep transcripts and saved translations in History on your device, where you can search, edit, tag, and delete them • Store your OpenRouter key in secure device storage SayCopy has no SayCopy account, subscription, advertising, cross-app tracking, or analytics SDK. Audio and text leave your device only when you request processing. Those requests are sent to OpenRouter and eligible model providers under their policies and your OpenRouter account settings. Read-aloud runs on your phone.

Reviewer instructions

SayCopy does not use a SayCopy account. Remote processing requires a temporary OpenRouter API key supplied for review. 1. Open Settings. 2. Paste the temporary review key in “OpenRouter token.” 3. Tap “Save token.” 4. Return to Record. 5. Tap Rec, allow microphone permission, speak a short phrase, and tap Stop. 6. The transcription appears and can be copied, shared, or opened from History. 7. To test translation, open the Translate tab, choose From and To languages, type or tap Speak to say a short phrase, then tap Translate. 8. The translation can be copied, played with Listen, or saved to Saved translations. The review key is limited and may be revoked after review. If it does not work, contact tarek@altitudeinfosys.com. Do not publish this key in the store listing.
